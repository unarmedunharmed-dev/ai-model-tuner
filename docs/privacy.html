Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

AI Model Tuner ("the App") is a desktop application for Windows. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is handled.

What Information We Collect

The App (Desktop Software): AI Model Tuner collects information about your computer's hardware to perform benchmarking and suggest optimal inference settings. This includes:

  • CPU model, core count, and architecture
  • GPU model and VRAM
  • Total system RAM
  • Windows version

This data is collected locally on your machine. It is not sent to any server, not stored remotely, and not shared with anyone. It is used solely to run benchmarks and display results within the app.

The Website: This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, page views) as described in GitHub's Privacy Policy. This site does not set cookies, use analytics trackers, or collect any personal data via forms.

Payments

Payments for the Pro version are processed through PayPal. AI Model Tuner does not receive or store your payment details. Please refer to PayPal's Privacy Policy for how your payment information is handled.

Email

If you email me at heine@online.no, your email address and message will be kept only for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. It will not be used for marketing or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following open-source libraries for local hardware detection:

  • systeminformation — retrieves hardware specs locally
  • electron-log — writes debug logs to your local machine (not transmitted)

Your Rights

Since the App does not collect or store any personal data on a server, there is nothing to access, modify, or delete. If you have questions, contact me at heine@online.no.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Continued use of the App or website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
